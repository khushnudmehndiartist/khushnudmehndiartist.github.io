About Khushnud Mehndi Artist

Weaving tradition into every intricate line, we specialize in creating breathtaking mehndi designs that celebrate your special moments.

With a passion for henna art and years of experience, Khushnud brings a unique blend of traditional Indian motifs and modern Arabic styles. Whether it's a grand wedding or an intimate gathering, our designs are tailored to reflect your personality and style.

  • 100% Organic Henna — Dark, Long-lasting Stain
  • Intricate & Neat Detailing
  • Customized Bridal Packages
  • On-Location Service Available
